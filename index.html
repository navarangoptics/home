FAQs

Quick answers

What are your opening hours?

All branches are open every day, 10:30 am to 9:30 pm, including holidays. No appointment is needed.

Is the eye test free?

Yes. A complimentary, computerised eye test is carried out by a certified optometrist, with no obligation to buy.

Which brands do you carry?

Thirty-three international brands, from everyday favourites to the finest luxury houses, with 2,500+ frames plus sunglasses and lenses.

What is the price range?

Budget-friendly choices start around Rs 2,000 to 5,000, branded eyewear from about Rs 8,000, and premium brands from Rs 30,000 and above.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes. A 1-year manufacturer warranty on frames, sunglasses and prescription lenses, plus free repairs wherever possible.
